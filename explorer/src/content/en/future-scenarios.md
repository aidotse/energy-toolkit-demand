## Future Scenarios

The future is not predetermined. Different development paths for electrification, economic growth, and technology development yield radically different outcomes for electricity demand.

### Scenario Descriptions

Our scenarios represent different possible futures:

- **Base pace**: Electrification continues at current rate
- **Rapid transition**: Accelerated electrification of all sectors
- **Limited growth**: Slower economic and technological development

Each scenario is built on clear assumptions about:
- Electrification rate in transport and industry
- Economic growth rate
- Technical efficiency improvements
- Behavioral changes

### Scenario Comparison

The differences between scenarios are particularly clear when looking at:
- Total consumption over time
- Peak load and power demand
- Geographic distribution
- Sector composition
