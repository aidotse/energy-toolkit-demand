## Welcome to Behovskartan

This is a tool for exploring and analyzing future electricity demand in Sweden.

### About the Projections

The projections are based on historical electricity consumption and apply various transformations to create future scenarios.

#### Methodology

Each scenario is defined by parameters such as:
- Electrification rate for different sectors
- Economic growth rate
- Transition speed to renewable energy sources

Results are presented as interactive visualizations showing:
- Time series development over years
- Geographic patterns
- Sector distribution
