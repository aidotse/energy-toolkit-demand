## Executive Summary

Sweden's electricity demand is facing a comprehensive transformation. The electrification of transportation, industry, and heating is driving a significant increase in demand while variation across different time periods is growing.

### Key Metrics

These key figures provide a quick overview of the changes:

- **Total consumption**: From current levels to future projections
- **Peak load**: Highest expected power demand
- **Growth rate**: Annual increase in demand
- **Sector distribution**: Share between industry, transport, and residential
