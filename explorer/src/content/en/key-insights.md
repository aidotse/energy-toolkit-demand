## Key Insights

The analyses point to several critical factors for Sweden's future electricity system.

### System Challenges

- **Power demand grows faster than energy**: Peak load increases more rapidly than total consumption
- **Geographic imbalance intensifies**: Some regions face disproportionately increased load
- **Time variation increases**: The difference between low and high load grows

### Planning Implications

These trends mean that:
- Grid capacity must be significantly expanded
- Flexibility and storage become increasingly important
- Regional coordination is critical
- Long-term planning must begin now
