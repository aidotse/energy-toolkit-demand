## Current State

To understand the future, we must first understand the present. Sweden's electricity consumption today is characterized by clear patterns across time, geography, and sectors.

### Consumption Patterns

Current electricity consumption varies significantly:

- **Over the year**: Higher consumption in winter due to heating
- **Over the day**: Clear peaks morning and evening
- **Between regions**: North has higher share of industry, south more residential
- **Between sectors**: Industry accounts for the largest share

These patterns form the basis for our future projections and help us understand how the transformation will impact the system.
